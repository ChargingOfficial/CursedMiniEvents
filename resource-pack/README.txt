Cursed Mini Events - Apple Of Life Resource Pack

Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.11

Install: Options > Resource Packs > Open Pack Folder, then place the ZIP in that folder and enable it. Do not extract the ZIP. The pack must be enabled by each player to see the custom Apple Of Life texture.

The plugin applies the cursedminievents:apple_of_life item model to Apple Of Life items. Vanilla golden apples are unchanged.
